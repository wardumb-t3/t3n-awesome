# Governance

Awesome Trusted AI is a community list stewarded by Terminal 3.

## Maintainers

Maintainers review pull requests, grant the **Secured on T3N** badge, and keep the list healthy.
They are listed in [`.github/CODEOWNERS`](.github/CODEOWNERS).

Anyone with three or more merged entries or substantial reviews can be nominated as a maintainer by
an existing maintainer. The goal is for at least one maintainer to be from outside Terminal 3.

## How decisions are made

- **Adding an entry**: one maintainer approves. For issue submissions, adding the `approved` label
  runs the submission bot, and a maintainer still reviews and merges the pull request it opens.
- **Granting Secured on T3N**: two maintainers approve after reproducing the WASM hash.
- **Removing an entry**: any maintainer, with the reason in the pull request. Entries are removed
  when they are abandoned for 12 months, their links break and aren't fixed within 30 days, or a
  security issue goes unaddressed.
- **Changing these rules**: a pull request to this file, open for at least seven days.

## Scope

Demos, workflows, tools and agents must be built on T3N; that's what this list is for. Learn is the
exception: it covers building safe agentic systems on any stack. All entries are judged on merit,
and Terminal 3 entries meet the same bar as everyone else's.
